Execution Model

The SaaS++ Framework

The AI-Native
Enterprise Layer

SaaS is the system of record. AI is the execution layer. BizApps is the orchestration and outcome layer that sits between them. Our L0 to L3 model defines how we embed intelligence at every depth across every platform we work with.

Each level compounds on the last. Engineering velocity at L0 makes everything above it faster. Platform activation at L1 unlocks immediate value. Extension at L2 pushes beyond native limits. And at L3, SaaS++ delivers outcomes that no single platform can achieve alone.

L3

SaaS++

AI-native solutions that transcend individual platforms. Cross-platform orchestration delivering outcomes no single SaaS can achieve alone.

L2

Extension

Custom agents, cross-system workflows, and bespoke AI models that push platforms beyond their native limits.

L1

Out of the Box

Native AI capabilities configured and activated to deliver immediate enterprise value from day one.

L0

Engineering AI

Developer productivity, code generation, documentation automation. The foundation that accelerates everything above it.
